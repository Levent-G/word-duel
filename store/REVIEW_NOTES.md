# App Review Information → Notes

Aşağıdaki metnin tamamını App Store Connect'te sürüm sayfasındaki **App Review Information → Notes** alanına yapıştır.
İnceleme ekibi soru sorarsa ("Guideline 2.1 Information Needed") aynı metni yanıt olarak da gönderebilirsin; bir iPhone ekran kaydı eklemek işi hızlandırır.

---

Hello App Review Team,

Thank you for reviewing Word Duel.

WHAT THE APP IS
Word Duel is an English vocabulary quiz game for Turkish speakers. Two players answer the same 10 multiple-choice questions at the same time; faster correct answers earn more points. Target audience: Turkish-speaking teenagers and adults learning English (CEFR levels A1–C2).

NO LOGIN REQUIRED
There is no registration, e-mail or password. On first launch the app asks only for a display name. For online play it signs in to Firebase Authentication anonymously in the background.

HOW TO TEST
1. Enter any name (for example "Reviewer") on the welcome dialog.
2. Home → "Bota Karşı Oyna" (Play vs bot) starts a full match immediately. This shows every gameplay feature without a second device: live opponent answers, the scoreboard, preset reactions (smiley button in the middle of the scoreboard), the result screen with the head-to-head comparison, rematch and the match summary.
3. Home → "Online Düello" (Online duel) looks for a real player at the same level. If nobody is online, a "Bota Karşı Oyna" button appears after 20 seconds. To see a real online match, use two devices with the same word level (Profile › Settings › Kelime seviyesi) and tap "Online Düello" on both.
4. "Arkadaşınla" (Play with a friend) → "Oda Aç" shows a room code; on a second device "Odaya Katıl" with that code starts a private, unrated match.
5. Profile › Sıralama (Leaderboard): online and bot leaderboards, and the weekly league table.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT AND SAFETY (Guideline 1.2)
The only user-generated content is the display name, which other players see in matches and leaderboards. There is no free-text chat; players can only send four preset reactions.
• Filtering: offensive Turkish and English names are rejected when entered and masked as "Oyuncu" when shown.
• Reporting: on the online match result screen tap "Rakibi bildir veya engelle", or long-press a player on the online leaderboard. Reports are stored in our database and reviewed by the developer.
• Blocking: the same sheet blocks the player; blocked players are never matched again and are hidden on leaderboards. Unblock in Profile › Settings › "Engellenen oyuncular".
• Contact: levent_gulsum@outlook.com (also on https://leventgulsum-word-duel.expo.app).

ACCOUNT AND DATA DELETION (Guideline 5.1.1(v))
Profile › Settings › "Hesabımı ve verilerimi sil" deletes the player's server data (name, rating, leagues, leaderboard entries, word history), the anonymous Firebase account and all local progress.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
Google Firebase Realtime Database and Firebase Authentication (anonymous) for matchmaking, live matches, leagues and leaderboards. No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. Pronunciation uses the on-device iOS text-to-speech. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device; no push server is used. The app uses only standard HTTPS encryption.

CONTENT
The vocabulary database (words, Turkish meanings, example sentences) is our own content. Sound effects are generated by us. Fonts are Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License. There are no in-app purchases and no ads.
